the one-line version

We keep the places you pin, the trips you plan, and the notes you scribble — only so your map shows up the same everywhere you sign in. That's the whole trick. Everything below is just us saying that more slowly.

what we hang onto

Your pins, collections, trips, notes, the visits you log — and the basics of your account: an email, a password we scramble into gibberish before we store it (we genuinely never see the real one), and a name if you feel like giving one. We keep this so your someday-map follows you from your phone to your laptop to that borrowed tablet on a rainy afternoon.

no account? still cool.

You can wander in as a guest, poke around the community map, and drop your own private pins — and those pins stay right there on your device. They never make the trip to our servers, which means there's nothing of yours for us to peek at, sync, or misplace. The catch is the fair one: pins that live only on this device won't teleport to your other ones. Want them to follow you? That's what an account is for.

what we count (spoiler: not you)

We do peek at anonymous, add-it-all-up numbers — like "the map got opened this many times today" — so we know whether wayfinder is actually useful or just vibes. But those are counts of moments, not people. As a guest you don't get a secret tracking tag, and we're not sketching a little profile of you or following your IP around. You're a happy blur in a total, and that's exactly how we like it.

stuff we'd never pull

  • Selling your data. Not to advertisers, not to anyone. It's yours.
  • Cramming ads into your map or letting someone target you here.
  • Sharing your private pins. The only things that go public are the ones you deliberately publish to the community — and that's always your move to make.

wherever you're wandering from

Someday-maps get made all over the world, so here's our promise regardless of which corner of it you're in: we treat everyone's data with the same respect. Keep as little as we can, be upfront about it, never sell it, and always let you walk away with your stuff. Those are the rules we hold ourselves to no matter where you signed up from.
