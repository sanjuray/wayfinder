The field guide

How Wayfinder works

Wayfinder is a map of the places you mean to go — and the ones you're glad you did. Pin a spot, give it a vibe, gather a few into a collection, then string them into a trip. This guide walks every corner of the app, and decodes every mark on the map.

02

The map

The map is home base. Every place you've saved lives here as a pin. The panel on the left filters what you see; the buttons floating over the map are how you add and search.

The blue dot

That pulsing blue dot is you — your device's current location, with a soft ring showing accuracy. Turn it off or set a fixed default in Settings → Advanced → Location.

Clusters

When pins pile up, they collapse into a numbered circle. Zoom in or click it to fan them back out.

The filter panel

Tap a category, vibe, or collection to narrow the map. Active filters stack — the pill over the map always says what's showing.

Double-click to pin

Double-click (or double-tap) any empty spot to start adding a place right there. Do it on the same spot twice for a small surprise.

03

Reading the pins

A pin isn't just a dot. Its colour, its badge, and its ring each carry a piece of information. Here's the whole vocabulary.

Colour & icon = category

Every pin is coloured by its category and stamped with that category's icon — a fork for Restaurant, a cup for Café, and so on. Same colours used in the filter panel.

Teal dot = planned

A small teal dot in the top-right corner means this is a place you plan to visit. No dot means you've already been — it's visited.

Gold ring = favourite

A warm gold halo around a pin marks it as a favourite. Star a place from its detail panel and it glows on the map.

Numbered circle = cluster

Several pins close together group into one circle showing how many are hidden inside. Click to zoom in and split them.

A pin can wear more than one mark at once — a gold-ringed, teal-dotted purple pin is a favourite temple you still plan to visit.
04

Adding a place

Adding a place is a short four-step flow. The progress dots at the top of the card show where you are.

  1. 1

    Where?

    Paste a Google Maps share link and it resolves automatically. Or paste any address — even a caption from a reel or short — and Wayfinder geocodes it. Adding from a double-click on the map skips this step.

  2. 2

    Name it

    Give the place a name you'll actually recognise later — "the dumpling place" beats its official title. If the pin landed slightly off, drag it on the mini-map to nudge it.

  3. 3

    What is it?

    Pick one category (this sets the pin's colour and icon), then add any number of vibe tags — date-worthy, hidden-gem, touristy, and so on.

  4. 4

    Save it where?

    Drop it into one or many collections — or none. Set it as planned or visited, and star it if it's a favourite. Type a new collection name right here to create one on the spot.

When you save, the new pin drops onto the map with a little celebration. That's Wayfinder's way of saying: noted.
05

A place up close

Click any pin and a panel slides in with everything about that place — and everything you can do with it.

Rename & delete

The pencil edits the name inline; the trash removes the place (with a confirm, so no accidents).

Status & favourite

Toggle between ○ planned and ✓ visited, or tap the star to favourite it — both update the pin instantly.

Vibes & collections

See at a glance which vibe tags and collections this place belongs to.

Visits & notes

Log a visit with a date, a rating — 👍 / 😐 / 👎 — and a line about how it went. Each place keeps its full history.

Open in Google Maps

The main button opens the place in Google Maps. The little chevron beside it opens a menu of query options — handy when Google's default guess lands in the wrong spot. Tick "set as default" and your chosen option sticks for that place, including in trip directions.

06

Categories & vibes

Two different ways to describe a place. Categories answer what is this?; vibes answer what's it like?

Categories

Each place has exactly one. It sets the pin's colour and icon. Wayfinder ships with a full set — Restaurant, Café, Bar, Landmark, Park, Beach, Stay, Temple, Trek, Viewpoint and more — and you can add, rename, recolour, or hide any of them in Settings.

Restaurant Café Park Temple Beach

Vibes

Free-floating tags a place can have any number of. They don't change how the pin looks, but you can filter the map by them and they show up in a place's detail. Great for cross-cutting moods that don't fit a single category.

07

Collections

A collection is a named set of places — "Tokyo 2026", "Coffee to try", "Where to take mum". A place can live in many collections at once.

The list

Every collection as a cover card with its own gradient and icon, its place count, and when you last touched it.

Inside a collection

Open one to see its places listed beside a map showing only those pins. Click a row to fly to it and open its detail — same feel as the main map.

Make it yours

Change the cover gradient or icon, rename it, or remove a place (with an undo if you change your mind).

Summary line

Each collection shows a quick tally: how many places, how many visited, how many favourites.

08

The places list

Prefer a list to a map? The Places page is every saved place as rows — sortable, and quick to scan.

Sort any way

By recently added, last visited, category, or favourites first. The sort menu remembers your pick.

Every row at a glance

Category icon and colour, name, planned/visited badge, vibe tags, and a favourite star — the same language as the map.

Bulk actions

Select several at once to change their status or clear favourites in one go.

Search from here

The search bar filters the list live as you type — by name, address, category, or vibe.

Trips

A trip strings places into an ordered route you can follow — and, once you're out there, tick off as you go.

The trips page

Your trips are grouped by where they stand:

In progress Upcoming Drafts & undated Past

Inside the planner

Build the route

Add stops from your saved places, then drag to reorder. The order is the route.

Distance & time

Between each pair of stops you get a rough distance and travel time, based on your chosen mode — walking, driving, transit, and so on.

Live trips

Start a trip and mark stops visited as you reach them. The line fills in teal behind you, and a marker rides along at your latest stop.

Open in Google Maps

Export the whole route as Google Maps directions with your stops as waypoints. Long trips split into a few links (Google caps waypoints per link).

11

Account & backups

Your places are saved as you go. How they travel between devices depends on whether you're signed in.

Signed in

Everything syncs to your account automatically and stays in step across every device you use. The save-status button in the top bar shows when the last sync happened, and you can sync on demand any time.

As a guest

Your data lives in this browser only. To move it to another device, export a backup file from Settings and import it on the other side. It's the guest's way to travel.

Export & import are available to everyone, signed in or not — a portable JSON snapshot of everything you've saved, from Settings → Storage & backup or the save-status menu.

Profile

Set a display name and a handle that travel with your account.

Plan & billing

Wayfinder has a free tier and a Circle upgrade. Manage it from Settings → Plan & billing.

Location

In Settings → Advanced, decide whether the map uses your live location, and set a default spot to fall back on.

Categories & vibes

Add, rename, recolour, or hide the tags you organise with — all in Settings.
